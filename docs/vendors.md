# Vendors

A vendor is an outside company or tradesperson hired for maintenance work.

- **Name** — Required, up to 100 characters, and unique ignoring case.
- **Trade** — Optional, up to 100 characters (for example, HVAC or plumbing).
- **Contact name** — Optional, up to 100 characters.
- **Email address** — Optional, up to 255 characters. When provided, it must be a valid email address.
- **Phone number** — Optional, up to 50 characters. When provided, it may contain digits, `+`, `-`, `.`, parentheses, and spaces, and must contain at least seven digits.
- **Notes** — Optional, up to 1,000 characters.
- **Active** — New vendors are active. Deactivate vendors that are no longer used.
