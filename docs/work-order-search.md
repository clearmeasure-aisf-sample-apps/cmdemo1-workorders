# Work Order Search

Use the filters on the Work Order Search page to narrow the list of work orders. Filters can be combined. The all/any option leaves a dropdown field unrestricted; turn off **Overdue only** to include work orders regardless of due date.

- **Creator** — Show work orders created by a selected person. **Anyone** includes every creator.
- **Assignee** — Show work orders assigned to a selected person. **Anyone** includes assigned and unassigned work orders. Use **Assigned to me** to limit results to the signed-in user.
- **Status** — Show work orders in a selected status, or choose **All statuses**. The available statuses are Draft, Assigned, In Progress, Complete, and Cancelled. Waiting on a vendor is an In Progress substate, not a separate status.
- **Overdue only** — Show open work orders whose due date is before today. Open work orders are Draft, Assigned, or In Progress; work orders without a due date are not overdue.
- **Priority** — Show work orders at a selected priority, or choose **All priorities**. Priorities are Low, Medium, and High; Medium is the default for new work orders.
- **Building** — Show work orders for a selected building, or choose **All buildings** to include work orders regardless of building, including work orders without one.

Active filters appear as removable chips. Select **Clear all** to reset the filters.
